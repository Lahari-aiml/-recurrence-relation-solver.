💡 Educational Guide

How the Substitution Method Works

The 6 rigorous mathematical steps used to solve recurrence relations without shortcuts.

1
📝

Write the Recurrence

State the original relation $T(n)$ along with the boundary condition $T(n_0) = b_0$.

$$T(n) = T(n-1) + 5$$
2
🔄

Substitute Repeatedly

Replace $T(n-1)$ or $T(n/b)$ with its formula for steps 1, 2, and 3 to reveal term growth.

$$T(n) = T(n-3) + 15$$
3
🔍

Find the Pattern

Express the equation after an arbitrary $k$ substitutions as a general $k$-th formula.

$$T(n) = T(n-k) + 5k$$
4
🎯

Reach Base Case

Set the inner subproblem equal to base index $n_0$ (e.g., $n-k = 1$) to solve for $k$.

$$k = n - 1$$
5
✨

Simplify Algebraic Form

Substitute $k$ and the base case value $T(1)$ back into the formula to produce the closed form.

$$T(n) = 5n - 4$$
6
📊

Determine Complexity

Identify the highest-order dominant term to formulate the tight asymptotic bound.

$$\Theta(n)$$
