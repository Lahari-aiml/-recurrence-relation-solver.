What is a Recurrence Relation?

A recurrence relation is an equation that defines a sequence recursively, expressing $T(n)$ in terms of smaller input sizes like $T(n-1)$ or $T(n/2)$. They are heavily used to analyze recursive algorithms.

How Substitution Method Works

The substitution method repeatedly expands the recurrence equation step-by-step until a recognizable geometric or algebraic pattern emerges after $k$ steps. Setting $k$ to reach the base case yields the closed-form solution.

Why Step-by-Step Explanations Matter

Simply knowing the final Big-$\Theta$ asymptotic bound isn't enough for university exams or deep understanding. Our solver shows every algebraic step so you learn *how* the exact formula is derived.
